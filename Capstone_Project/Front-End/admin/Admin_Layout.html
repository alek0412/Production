Layout

Control how general (public) and customer pages look and feel—spacing, imagery, and presentation so the experience stays clear and appealing. Below: Upcoming events on the shared home dashboards (images only—common web and photo formats such as JPG, PNG, WebP, GIF, SVG, HEIC, and AVIF, up to about 50 MB each; not PDF), the Popular Times sheet (PDF or images) on both Availability pages, membership pricing (PDF or images) on General and Client Membership, and the Gallery on About (PDF or images, up to 20 slots).

01 — Home

Home/Dashboard - Modify Upcoming Events

Hero imagery on the shared general and client home dashboards.

One image uses a large layout on the home page; two or more use a smaller shared grid. Drag a photo or graphic onto a card (the border highlights), or use Image file, then Save image. Use standard image types in common use today (e.g. JPG, PNG, WebP, GIF, SVG, HEIC, AVIF)—not PDF. For a PDF, use Availability, Membership pricing, or Gallery below. Nothing is uploaded until you save. After you save, the preview shows that same image the next time you open Layout (even after signing out).

02 — Availability

Availability - Modify Availability Times

Popular Times on both Availability experiences.

This appears on General and Client Availability. Upload a PDF or image (PNG, JPG, WebP, SVG, HEIC, AVIF, and other common formats—up to about 50 MB). Click Save image to keep it—saved files show in this preview when you return. Remove hides the Popular Times block on those pages until you save a new file.

03a — Membership

Memberships - Modify Specials

Each block has a Name of Special and a Description. New lines in the description become new lines on the site.

03b — Membership

Memberships - Modify Membership Pricing

Pricing sheet on General and Client Membership.

This appears on General and Client Membership. Upload a PDF or image (PNG, JPG, WebP, SVG, HEIC, AVIF, and most other common formats—up to about 50 MB). Unsupported types are rejected. Click Save image to keep it—the preview here reflects what is saved when you come back. Remove hides the pricing block on those pages until you save a new file.

Membership pricing

04 — Gallery

Gallery — A look inside HBC

Snapshots from Houston Badminton Center — courts, programs, and community.

This appears on General and Client About. Choose how many slots you use (up to 20 total). Each slot accepts PDF or a common image (JPG, PNG, WebP, GIF, SVG, HEIC, AVIF, etc.—about 50 MB each; types the server cannot identify are rejected). Click Save on each slot so the file is kept—those previews show again the next time you open Layout. Until you upload files, the Gallery section shows a short placeholder on the public About pages. Remove clears one slot; Remove all pictures clears every slot.

You can have at most 20 files. Reducing the count deletes files in the removed slots.
